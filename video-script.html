How we show the app

Three kinds of shot, mixed on purpose. Filmed shots prove it's real, mockups show the app clearly, and punch-ins make one detail land. Never show the same screen the same way twice in a row.

Filmed

A real phone in real hands

Over the shoulder, phone in focus, the donation behind it. Use for the scan and anything physical. Cut from the phone screen into a mockup on the same frame (a match cut).

Mockup

Screen recording in a PromoGen phone

Record on the iPhone, slow and deliberate: hold 2 seconds before and after each tap. Drop the clip into a PromoGen scene. Use for flows like Detect, Confirm and Share.

Punch-in

Zoom on one detail

Crop the mockup to a single element with a short caption, held for 2 to 3 seconds. Use for the numbers that matter: “6 boxes”, “Protein runs out first”, “13d left”.

Before recording

The app Capture

  • Rename the test pantry to something real-sounding (it currently reads “Emulator Test Pantry”).
  • Take a few items off the shelf as “given out” so the Reports chart has bars.
  • Hide Expo Go's floating gear button in its dev menu, or it shows up in every recording.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb and charge the phone, so no banners or low-battery icon appear.
  • Record each flow three times, slowly. Hold 2 seconds before and after every tap.

The shoot Director

  • Props: a clipboard and tally sheet, a donation pile with real labels, an empty box to pack.
  • Shoot in daylight or by a window. Hold the phone steady and lock focus on the screen.
  • Record the voiceover separately, in a closet or small room, with the phone 15 cm from your mouth.
  • Rough-cut the hook first. If it works, the rest of the video follows.
  • Over time? Cut from “Built for real pantries”, never from the hook or the ending.
